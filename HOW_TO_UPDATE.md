# How to update this profile

This repository has nothing to run. GitHub shows `README.md` at the top of the profile page github.com/agshivam.

## Change the text

1. Open `README.md` on GitHub and click the pencil icon.
2. Edit, then choose **Commit changes**.
3. Reload github.com/agshivam. The change shows within a minute.

## When a project is finished

In the "Showcase projects" table of `README.md`:
1. Change its **Status** from "Planned" to "Live".
2. Make the project name a link to its repo, like `[Job Radar](https://github.com/agshivam/job-radar)`.
3. Add its live Streamlit link in the "What it shows" column if you have one.

## Pins and bio (done on your profile, not in this repo)

1. Open github.com/agshivam and click **Customize your pins**. Pick your best 4 to 6 repos, with `patient-360` first.
2. Click **Edit profile** and add a bio such as "Senior Data Engineer: Snowflake, dbt, Fivetran". Add your location and a clear photo.
